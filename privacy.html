Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Lecture Vault is a personal, non-commercial tool. It lets me, and a few people I know personally, access encrypted lecture videos stored on Google Drive. This policy explains what happens to your data when you sign in with Google.

What Google data the app accesses

When you sign in, Lecture Vault asks for permission to access files in your Google Drive. It needs this to list, upload, and read the encrypted lecture files. People who are only watching lectures are asked for read-only access.

How the data is used

Access is used only to list and stream or upload those files. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

All processing happens on your own device. Your sign-in token is stored only in the configuration file on that device. I operate no server, database, or analytics service, so I never receive your files, your account details, or your sign-in token.

Sharing

I do not sell, share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to anyone. Because I never receive it, there is nothing for me to share.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lecture Vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Because I hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for me to delete. To stop access at any time, remove Lecture Vault at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the configuration file on your device.
