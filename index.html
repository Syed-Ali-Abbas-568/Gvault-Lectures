Lecture Vault

A personal tool for keeping my own lecture videos encrypted on Google Drive and streaming them on my phone.

What it does

Lecture Vault connects the open-source tool rclone to my Google Drive. Videos are encrypted on my computer before upload, so Google only stores scrambled files. When I watch a lecture, it is decrypted on my own device while it streams.

Who it is for

Only me and a small number of people I know personally, who sign in with their own Google accounts to watch lectures I have shared with them. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

Your data

Lecture Vault runs entirely on each person's own devices. I run no servers and receive no data from anyone who uses it. Read the privacy policy for details.
